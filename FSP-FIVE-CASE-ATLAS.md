# FSP FIVE-CASE ATLAS

## Foucault · Neptune/Vulcan · Galileo · Newton→Einstein · DESI 2024→2026

**Status:** Canonical comparative backbone for the encyclopedia sequence  
**Purpose:** Encode five historically distinct scientific cases through one FSP diachronic inquiry grammar without flattening their differences.

## 1. Shared formal object

Each dossier is represented as a sequence of inquiry states:

[
\mathcal I_t=
\langle
D,C,E,W,A,B,M,O,T,S,P,X,R,\Gamma,F
\rangle
]

where:

- **D** — situated Doing;
- **C** — claims;
- **E** — evidence;
- **W** — warrants;
- **A** — auxiliary assumptions;
- **B** — background commitments;
- **M** — meanings / conceptual organization;
- **O** — ontology commitments;
- **T** — task;
- **S** — standing;
- **P** — power / authority structure;
- **X** — live possibilities / revision frontier;
- **R** — unresolved or translation remainder;
- **Γ** — claim ceiling;
- **F** — framework.

A transition is encoded as:

[
\mathcal I_t
\xrightarrow{\Delta}
\mathcal I_{t+1}
]

with:

[
\Delta=
\langle
\Delta D,\Delta C,\Delta E,\Delta W,\Delta A,\Delta B,
\Delta M,\Delta O,\Delta T,\Delta S,\Delta P,\Delta X,
\Delta R,\Delta\Gamma,\Delta F
\rangle.
]

The Atlas does not require every dimension to change in every case.

That is the point.

## 3. Foucault dossier

### Canonical question

> What did Foucault's air/water light-speed result discriminate, and what did it not uniquely establish?

### State F0 — rival optical systems

The experiment is designed against rival articulated prediction structures.

The critical FSP distinction is:

[
\text{discriminating experiment}
\neq
\text{ontologically final experiment}.
]

### State F1 — experimental result

The observed slower propagation in water breaks the represented predictive tie in favor of the wave-system relation.

Changed dimensions:

[
\Delta E,\Delta W,\Delta X,\Delta\Gamma.
]

### State F2 — Duhemian reconstruction

The experimental success is preserved, but the metaphysical ceiling is contracted.

Changed dimensions:

[
\Delta W,\Delta B,\Delta M,\Delta O,\Delta R,\Delta\Gamma.
]

The result remains powerful without being promoted to proof that every possible alternative ontology is impossible.

### Controlled probe

Hold the measured air/water relation fixed.

Vary the auxiliary assumptions connecting an emission ontology to its predicted medium-speed relation.

The exercise asks whether logical reopening of a reconstructed rival is possible without pretending that the original rival regains equal empirical standing.

## 4. Neptune/Vulcan dossier

### Canonical question

> Why can the same auxiliary-first revision strategy succeed for one anomaly and fail for another?

### NV0 → NV1

Uranus's anomaly creates revision pressure.

Instead of revising the Newtonian framework immediately, inquiry changes an auxiliary:

[
\Delta A + \Delta X.
]

### NV1 → NV2

The new-body prediction gains observational support.

The auxiliary repair succeeds.

### NV2 → NV3

The same broad repair topology is reused for Mercury.

But past success does not transfer automatically.

### NV3 → NV4

Vulcan's failed evidential trajectory is followed by deeper framework change.

The transition now includes:

[
\Delta M+\Delta O+\Delta F.
]

The pair therefore blocks both:

> Always repair auxiliaries first.

and:

> Persistent anomaly requires immediate framework replacement.

## 5. Galileo dossier

### Canonical question

> How did observational evidence, conceptual change, interpretive standing, and institutional power interact in the Copernican controversy?

### G0 → G1

Telescopic observation changes:

[
E,W,A,M,O,X,\Gamma.
]

The older celestial/terrestrial divide comes under pressure.

### G1 → G2

The controversy expands.

The new state now prominently includes:

[
T,S,P,R.
]

The issue is no longer only what the telescope shows.

It includes:

- what counts as demonstration;
- who has interpretive standing;
- which institutional authority governs which domain.

### G2 → G3

Institutional/legal power constrains what Galileo may publish and defend.

This changes:

[
D,S,P,X,\Gamma
]

without converting coercive authority into epistemic demonstration.

### Controlled probe

Hold the telescopic observations fixed.

Vary the cosmological framework used to accommodate them.

This reveals that strong evidence can constrain a theory space without uniquely selecting a complete cosmology.

## 6. Newton→Einstein dossier

### Canonical question

> How can a later theory reorganize core meanings and ontology while preserving important empirical structure from an earlier framework?

### RE0 — Newtonian framework

Mass, time, space, force, gravitation, and motion occupy a classical inferential network.

### RE0 → RE1 — special relativity

The transition includes:

[
\Delta M+\Delta O+\Delta F+\Delta R.
]

The same vocabulary does not guarantee unchanged theoretical meaning.

Yet many Newtonian predictions survive approximately in bounded regimes.

Thus:

[
\text{conceptual change}
\neq
\text{total empirical discontinuity}.
]

### RE1 → RE2 — equivalence-principle bridge

A controlled comparison between accelerated and gravitational descriptions creates local task-equivalence.

The remainder is crucial:

> tidal effects.

Local equivalence is therefore not global identity.

### RE2 → RE3 — general relativity

The gravitational framework changes again.

Newtonian results are not discarded wholesale; they are bounded within a different conceptual and ontological structure.

## 7. DESI 2024→2026 dossier

### Canonical question

> How should a scientific framework represent changing evidence when the live alternatives and claim ceiling move over successive data releases?

### D0 — April 2024

DESI Year 1 is broadly compatible with ΛCDM while combined analyses show a slight preference for time-varying dark-energy models.

The live frontier contains:

- ΛCDM;
- (w_0w_a)CDM / dynamic-dark-energy models;
- other extensions.

### D0 → D1 — March 2025

More DESI data and combined external datasets strengthen the dynamic-dark-energy preference.

The transition changes:

[
\Delta E+\Delta W+\Delta A+\Delta X+\Delta R+\Delta\Gamma.
]

But the claim ceiling still forbids:

> evolving dark energy has been discovered.

### D1 → D2 — July 2026

A more precise Lyman-alpha full-shape analysis shifts its central value toward ΛCDM.

This does not erase 2025.

Instead it changes the inquiry state again.

The correct representation is:

[
\mathcal I_{2024}
\rightarrow
\mathcal I_{2025}
\rightarrow
\mathcal I_{2026},
]

not:

[
\text{wrong}
\rightarrow
\text{right}
\rightarrow
\text{wrong}.
]

The sequence is a live example of evidence reweighting and revision-frontier movement.

## 8. Controlled revision spaces

Every dossier contains at least one controlled-revision probe.

These probes use the same grammar:

### Hold fixed

What is preserved for the exercise?

### Vary

What assumption, model, concept, standing relation, or framework is deliberately changed?

### Constraints

What may not be altered without changing the task?

### Candidate consequences

What follows in the represented possibility space?

### Remainder

What is still unresolved?

### Claim ceiling

What may the exercise license us to say?

This connects the Atlas directly to the thought-experiment chapter.

## 10. Formation and justification

The Atlas also preserves:

[
\text{formation}
\neq
\text{justification}.
]

Historical state transitions explain how an inquiry reached its current configuration.

They do not automatically justify the later theory, institution, vocabulary, or claim.

Likewise:

[
\text{later success}
\neq
\text{permission to rewrite earlier inquiry as irrational}.
]

A canonical dossier should preserve what was live at the historical moment.

## 11. Claim ceiling for the Atlas

The strongest current Atlas claim is:

> FSP can encode historically and scientifically distinct inquiry episodes through a shared diachronic grammar of states, transition dimensions, controlled revision spaces, claim ceilings, and REOPEN conditions while preserving their different evidential, semantic, institutional, and theoretical topologies.

The Atlas does not establish:

- that the FSP field decomposition is unique;
- that every historical fact fits neatly into the schema;
- that one formal metalanguage removes translation loss;
- that retrospective encoding reproduces the first-person experience of historical actors;
- that common representation implies common ontology.

## 12. Four-Move Governance Overlay

Each canonical dossier may expose the same governance strip without changing its historical topology.

### Foucault

- **Portability:** what moves from experimental discrimination into a claim about rival optical theories?
- **Ceiling:** what does the terrestrial light-speed result establish, and what does it not settle by itself?
- **Authorization:** what inferential authority should later historians or philosophers grant the experiment?
- **Return:** how do later theoretical developments change the significance of the result?
- **Remainder:** which theoretical and historical questions remain open?

### Neptune / Vulcan

- **Portability:** when may anomaly-resolution success travel from one planetary case to another?
- **Ceiling:** successful auxiliary introduction in one case ≠ warranted auxiliary in every analogous case.
- **Authorization:** what evidential authority should an anomaly be granted in revising theory or adding entities?
- **Return:** how does general relativity reopen the earlier settlement?
- **Remainder:** what could not be resolved within the inherited Newtonian framework?

### Galileo

- **Portability:** what moves from telescopic observation into cosmological and institutional claims?
- **Ceiling:** observation alone does not settle every interpretive or theological conclusion.
- **Authorization:** observational expertise ≠ unrestricted institutional authority.
- **Return:** how do later evidential and institutional developments alter the settlement?
- **Remainder:** what interpretive and standing conflicts remain?

### Newton → Einstein

- **Portability:** which Newtonian results survive into a changed conceptual framework?
- **Ceiling:** limiting recovery ≠ theoretical identity.
- **Authorization:** predictive success does not by itself decide every ontological interpretation.
- **Return:** later tests and theory development revise the successor framework.
- **Remainder:** what remains open about ontology, interpretation, and regime boundaries?

### DESI 2024 → 2026

- **Portability:** how do detector, calibration, statistical model, and cosmological inference carry warrant across a long measurement chain?
- **Ceiling:** high bridge burden ≠ weak inference; current evidence ≠ final cosmological closure.
- **Authorization:** cosmological inference does not automatically authorize broader metaphysical claims.
- **Return:** successive releases update the live alternatives and claim ceiling.
- **Remainder:** model dependence, calibration burden, and unresolved cosmological alternatives remain explicit.

### Overlay firewall

> **Shared governance questions ≠ historical sameness.**

The overlay is comparative metadata. It does not alter canonical states, source ledgers, transitions, or case-specific claim ceilings.
