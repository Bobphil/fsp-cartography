# FSP-ENCYCLOPEDIA-DOMAIN-DEEPENING-1

## Ethics, Institutions & Connected Doing, and Inquiry & Science

**Status:** PUBLIC CONTENT DEEPENING  
**Purpose:** Turn three domain landing pages from inventories into explanatory entrances anchored by worked Joint Task Spaces.

---

# 1. Common domain pattern

Each domain page should answer four questions:

1. **What does this domain make visible?**
2. **What does it contribute to a Joint Task Space?**
3. **What does it not own?**
4. **Where can the reader enter through a worked case?**

This is a public-facing pattern, not a new formal layer.

Firewall:

> **Domain contribution ≠ domain sovereignty over the whole task.**

---

# 2. Ethics

## What Ethics makes visible

Ethics attends to:

- reasons for action;
- duties and permissions;
- harm and benefit;
- dignity and respect;
- agency and autonomy;
- standing;
- fairness;
- dependency;
- capability;
- legitimacy;
- power;
- the distribution of burdens and benefits.

Ethics is not added only after empirical facts are settled.

Ethical questions often shape what counts as a relevant fact, whose experience must be represented, which alternatives deserve consideration, and what kinds of authorization require justification.

## What Ethics contributes to a Joint Task Space

Ethics contributes:

- standing analysis;
- burden visibility;
- legitimacy questions;
- claim ceilings on benefit-based reasoning;
- capability analysis;
- reasons for exception;
- justification of limits;
- normative conflict made explicit rather than hidden.

In Gulf Pines, Ethics asks not merely whether a visitation restriction reduces infection risk, but:

- who bears the restriction;
- whose agency is constrained;
- whose family relationship performs care;
- who may authorize the burden;
- whether equal rules create unequal capability losses;
- when emergency justification expires.

## What Ethics does not own

Ethics does not determine:

- infection probability;
- staffing capacity;
- legal authority by itself;
- epidemiological causation;
- operational feasibility;
- the empirical success of an intervention.

Firewall:

> **Moral relevance ≠ empirical determination.**

And:

> **Ethical judgment ≠ unrestricted authorization.**

## Worked entrance

**Gulf Pines** is the current canonical Ethics/Institutions Joint Task Space.

It shows how ethical standing, capability, burden, legitimacy, and repair remain connected to clinical, operational, family, regulatory, and institutional evidence without being reduced to them.

# 3. Institutions & Connected Doing

## What Institutions makes visible

Institutions stabilize and amplify human action.

They create:

- roles;
- permissions;
- procedures;
- defaults;
- metrics;
- incentives;
- reporting channels;
- authority;
- resource flows;
- enforcement;
- memory;
- persistence.

They can expand capability.

They can also sediment outdated judgments.

## What Institutions contributes to a Joint Task Space

This domain contributes analysis of:

- practical power;
- formal authority;
- role-bounded responsibility;
- implementation capacity;
- institutional amplification;
- policy sedimentation;
- feedback channels;
- regulatory learning;
- exception structures;
- multi-level coordination.

Gulf Pines shows the full loop:

```text
resident / frontline event
→ facility judgment
→ policy
→ implementation effects
→ facility review
→ higher-level learning
→ regulatory REOPEN
→ changed local environment
```

## What Institutions does not own

Institutional authority does not by itself settle:

- empirical truth;
- moral legitimacy;
- resident experience;
- scientific explanation;
- domain-native meaning.

Firewall:

> **Power ≠ legitimacy.**

And:

> **Operational authority ≠ moral completeness.**

## Worked entrance

**Gulf Pines Layer 5** is the canonical current entrance.

It shows how local judgments become rules and how local implementation effects may travel back upward into institutional and regulatory REOPEN.

# 4. Inquiry & Science

## What Inquiry & Science makes visible

Scientific inquiry makes visible:

- observation;
- measurement;
- models;
- prediction;
- causal hypotheses;
- uncertainty;
- error;
- experimental and observational constraints;
- model comparison;
- temporal revision.

The FSP Inquiry & Science collection also asks how scientific meanings and problem structures change over time.

## What Inquiry & Science contributes to a Joint Task Space

It contributes:

- evidence provenance;
- observation/inference separation;
- uncertainty representation;
- model plurality;
- causal restraint;
- controlled probes;
- comparison of competing explanations;
- sensitivity to diachronic change.

In Asteria-2047, scientific inquiry does not produce one direct policy answer.

It produces a changing probabilistic representation:

```text
observation
→ orbit estimate
→ impact probability
→ risk corridor
→ consequence model
→ candidate intervention analysis
```

Every transition is inspectable.

## What Inquiry & Science does not own

Scientific expertise does not by itself determine:

- legitimate risk transfer;
- standing;
- international authorization;
- acceptable burden;
- moral priority;
- political legitimacy.

Firewall:

> **Model success ≠ ontology.**

And:

> **Impact-model expertise ≠ mission authorization.**

## Worked entrances

**Asteria-2047** is the current Joint Task Space comparator entrance.

The **Scientific Atlas** remains the deeper historical/diachronic entrance through Foucault, Neptune/Vulcan, Galileo, Newton→Einstein, and DESI.

Together they show two roles for Inquiry & Science:

1. understanding how inquiry changes;
2. participating responsibly in cross-domain action.

# 5. Cross-domain relation

The three domains should not be presented as stacked authorities.

A Joint Task Space is better represented as:

```text
domain-native inquiry
↕
translation with provenance + remainder
↕
shared task representation
↕
standing / repair / authorization
↕
action and institutional effects
↕
REOPEN
```

Ethics may challenge what counts as acceptable burden.

Science may change the risk estimate.

Institutions may reveal that an authorized plan is not implementable.

Frontline evidence may reopen the shared map.

No domain is permanently upstream or downstream.

---

# 6. Public reader rule

Each domain page should expose:

- a plain-language domain purpose;
- a “what this domain sees” section;
- a “what it contributes” section;
- a “what it does not own” boundary;
- one or more worked entrances;
- current resources;
- current development boundary.

This structure should be reused for future domain deepening without forcing identical domain-native navigation.

---

# 7. Claim ceiling

This deepening does not claim that Ethics, Institutions, and Inquiry & Science are now complete Encyclopedia domains.

It gives each a coherent public entrance and connects mature or emerging resources to the larger FSP architecture.

> **Public coherence ≠ domain completion.**
