# FSP-HUMAN-FIDELITY-1A — Participant Information and Consent Sheet

## What is this session?

You are being invited to try a research prototype that helps a person map and reflect on a situation they are already dealing with.

The purpose is to learn whether the experience preserves your own situation, words, uncertainty, and judgment.

It is **not** designed to diagnose you, evaluate you, or make a decision for you.

## What will I do?

You will choose a real situation from your own life that is meaningful enough to explore but does not require sensitive disclosure.

You will move through seven views:

Start, People, Institution, H/S/F, Connections, Investigate, and Return.

You may skip any question or leave any section sparse or empty.

Afterward, you will be asked about:

- whether the map still felt like your situation;
- whether your own words were preserved;
- whether any part felt recognizable;
- whether any part felt like filling out a form;
- whether the final judgment felt like yours;
- what was missing or distorted.

## Do I have to disclose private information?

No.

Please do not provide medical, legal, sexual, financial, employment, or other sensitive details unless a separately approved study explicitly permits that.

## Is participation voluntary?

Yes.

You may decline any question, skip any section, or stop the session at any time.

You do not need to reach a final answer.

## Will the system decide for me?

It should not.

If the system's wording is wrong, you should correct it.

Your correction should take priority.

## Is this institutional or IRB approval?

No.

This information sheet is part of the research prototype packet. It does not by itself establish institutional ethics approval or permission to recruit participants under any institution's policies.
