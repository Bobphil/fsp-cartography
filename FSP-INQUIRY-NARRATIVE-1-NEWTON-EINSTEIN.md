# FSP-INQUIRY-NARRATIVE-1 — Newton→Einstein Third-Case Stress Test

## Purpose

Use Newton→Einstein to test a third inquiry topology.

Neptune/Vulcan pressures the architecture through divergent repair trajectories.

Galileo pressures it through coupled evidence, meaning, standing, jurisdiction, and institutional power.

Newton→Einstein pressures it through a different tension:

**deep conceptual transformation + preserved bounded empirical success.**

The architecture must represent both without collapsing into either total continuity or total replacement.

## Whole-inquiry narrative

The narrative carries the reader from the long-standing success of Newtonian mechanics through:

- 1905 special relativity;
- the reorganization of simultaneity, space, and time;
- the unresolved problem of gravitation;
- 1907–1911 equivalence-principle reasoning;
- 1915 general relativity and Mercury;
- 1919 and later observational testing;
- continuing Newtonian usefulness in weak-field / low-velocity regimes.

## Four apertures

### Chapter 1

Revision pressure acts on a highly successful framework; later relativistic success does not imply that one revision path was logically mandatory in advance.

### Chapter 2

Same words do not guarantee same inferential roles, yet meaning change does not entail total incomparability.

### Chapter 3

Simultaneity and elevator thought experiments clarify dependencies while preserving locality, remainder, and the distinction between conceptual insight and empirical confirmation.

### Chapter 4

The diachronic formalism must track changed meanings, ontology, and framework together with preserved low-velocity, weak-field, and local relations.

## Stress-tested firewalls

- later theory ≠ simple deletion of earlier theory;
- preserved empirical success ≠ unchanged conceptual meaning;
- same term ≠ same inferential role;
- conceptual discontinuity ≠ total incomparability;
- approximation/limiting recovery ≠ global theoretical identity;
- local equivalence ≠ global ontological identity;
- thought-experiment insight ≠ empirical confirmation.

## Claim ceiling

This extension tests whether the narrative-spine architecture can preserve structured continuity across conceptual change.

It does not settle the philosophy of reduction, correspondence, reference, incommensurability, or theory identity. Those remain REOPENable.
