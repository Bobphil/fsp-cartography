Before you begin

You are looking at possible things that might become possible from a historical situation. Some are available sooner; others require more things to change first. Explore the display as it seems natural to you.

Please do not ask the observer to explain interface terms while you are exploring. Confusion is useful information for this pilot.

Fixed sequence

1. Freeze. In the visualization, choose Freeze.
What possibilities seem available from here?
2. Accelerate. Choose Accelerate.
What changed?
3. Inspect advocacy. Select Publicly advocate for change.
Why does this possibility seem farther away than the ones near the center?
4. Pin and Freeze. Pin the advocacy path, then choose Freeze again.
What happened to the advocacy possibility?

The observer will ask several follow-up questions after Step 4. Do not accelerate again unless asked.
