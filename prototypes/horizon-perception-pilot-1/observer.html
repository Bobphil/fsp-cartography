Orientation: You are looking at possible things that might become possible from a historical situation. Some are available sooner; others require more things to change first. Explore the display as it seems natural to you.

  1. Freeze: “What possibilities seem available from here?”
  2. Accelerate: “What changed?”
  3. Inspect advocacy: “Why does this possibility seem farther away than the ones near the center?”
  4. Pin + Freeze: “What happened to the advocacy possibility?”
  5. “Did anything about the possibility itself change?”
  6. “Is it now easier to reach, harder to reach, or unchanged?”
  7. “Is it now more likely, less likely, or unchanged?”
  8. “Why do you think it moved visually?”
  9. “Without accelerating again, what would have to change before it became more reachable?”

Transfer: A = available now; B = requires several missing conditions; C = we know very little. Ask: “Which is most like the retained advocacy marker?”
