## Certified narrative role

FSP-INQUIRY-NARRATIVE-1 adds a whole-inquiry orientation layer between canonical case data and local chapter apertures.

The governing rule is:

`WHOLE INQUIRY → CHAPTER APERTURE → LOCAL INSPECTION → LOCAL RETURN → WHOLE-INQUIRY RETURN`

No local FSP inspection should require the reader to reconstruct the entire inquiry from disconnected fragments.

## First certified narrative spine

Case:

`NEPTUNE-VULCAN`

Title:

`Neptune and Vulcan: One Repair Strategy, Two Historical Trajectories`

The spine contains a continuous six-paragraph account carrying the reader through:

- the Uranus discrepancy under highly successful Newtonian celestial mechanics;
- the Adams / Le Verrier unseen-planet calculations;
- Neptune's September 1846 observational discovery;
- Le Verrier's 1859 Mercury residual and Vulcan repair;
- the extended unsuccessful Vulcan search trajectory;
- the 1915 general-relativistic explanation of Mercury's anomalous perihelion;
- the bounded philosophical conclusion that no universal auxiliary-first or universal framework-first rule follows.

## Temporal anchoring

The narrative introduces explicit dated milestones.

Formal states and transitions may point to these milestones.

The certified firewall is:

`FSP state order is anchored in historical time but is not identical with calendar time.`

A formal state may represent an extended inquiry condition.

A dated historical milestone does not become a canonical state merely because it is attached to one.

Later successful explanations may not be projected backward as if they were available to earlier investigators.

- relation to the whole inquiry;
- what FSP adds;

## Problem-context recovery

Every local view can recover four orientation questions:

1. **Whole** — What is the entire inquiry problem?
2. **Temporal** — Where are we historically, and what later evidence is not yet available?
3. **Local** — Which part of the larger problem are we inspecting?
4. **FSP** — What distinction, dependency, or boundary is FSP making easier to see?

The executable `recoverInquiryContext` helper returns these together with the whole-inquiry route and, where applicable, the local reader route.

## Whole↔part return

Two return scales are certified.

### Local Return

Restore the exact chapter sentence or inspection origin.

### Whole-Inquiry Return

Restore the case narrative spine with the relevant temporal and chapter context.

The whole-inquiry return complements exact Authorial Return; it does not replace it.

## Claim ceiling

This certification establishes the whole-inquiry narrative architecture for Neptune/Vulcan.

It does not yet certify equivalent narrative spines for Foucault, Galileo, Newton→Einstein, or DESI.

It also does not establish that the present Neptune/Vulcan narrative is the final historical treatment. Further historical scholarship, source improvement, or public-reader evidence may REOPEN wording, emphasis, milestone granularity, or source choice while preserving the constitutional whole↔part architecture.
