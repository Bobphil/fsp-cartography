# 2. Lemma 1 — Layer Non-Identity

Given:
    EXPERIENCE ≠ MECHANISM ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ METAPHYSICAL TRUTH

then:
> **Every cross-layer claim requires an explicit bridge.**

# 3. Lemma 2 — Noetic Non-Entailment

Given:
    experienced certainty ≠ epistemic warrant

then:
> **Noetic intensity alone cannot increase the epistemic authorization of a metaphysical claim.**

# 4. Lemma 3 — Context Underdetermination

Given:
    same dose ≠ same experience
    same context ≠ same PRF

then:
> **Compound and dose alone underdetermine acute phenomenal outcome.**

# 5. Lemma 4 — State/Trait Non-Identity

Given:
    acute state ≠ persistent trait
    near transfer ≠ durable transformation

then:
> **Acute phenomenology alone cannot license a durable PRF-transformation claim.**

# 6. Lemma 5 — Meaning/Truth Separation

Given:
    meaning ≠ factual accuracy

then:
> **A gain in personal meaning can be genuine while the truth status of the associated metaphysical interpretation remains unresolved.**

# 7. Lemma 6 — Mechanism Does Not Cancel Significance

Given:
    mechanistic explanation ≠ phenomenological invalidation

then:
> **A successful mechanistic account does not entail that the experience lacks existential, normative, or authorial significance.**

# 8. Lemma 7 — Ontological Non-Privilege

Given FSP's Ontological Non-Privilege Principle:

> **Neither ordinary waking ontology nor acute transcendent ontology receives automatic metaphysical privilege merely because one is baseline and the other is intense.**

# 9. Lemma 8 — Bridge Independence

For a chain:
    pharmacology
    → network dynamics
    → predictive organization
    → phenomenology
    → noetic interpretation
    → metaphysical claim

> **Validation of an earlier bridge does not automatically validate later bridges.**

# 10. Lemma 9 — PRF Exceeds Predictive Precision

Because PRF includes salience, history, social relations, normative orientation, identity, representation, and capability:

> **A precision change may contribute to PRF transformation without exhausting PRF transformation.**

# 11. Lemma 10 — Integration Is Not Epistemic Guarantee

Given:
    integration ≠ indoctrination
    meaning consolidation ≠ truth consolidation

then:
> **Integration may influence sedimentation without licensing the truth of what becomes sedimented.**

# 12. Conditional Theorem 1 — Context-Amplified Revision

If:
1. selected high-level belief confidence is measurably relaxed;
2. sensitivity to context-dependent evidence increases;
3. post-acute integration reinforces selected interpretations;
4. delayed measures show persistent belief change;

then:
> **State→trait belief revision is jointly dependent on perturbation and context rather than pharmacology alone.**

Status: CONDITIONAL EMPIRICAL THEOREM.

# 13. Conditional Theorem 2 — State→Trait Sedimentation

If:
1. acute experience produces a stable AssemblyTrace;
2. integration reinforces that trace;
3. later tasks reveal altered salience, discrimination, or action;
4. the change persists across contexts;

then:
> **A PRF-level sedimentation claim is licensed rather than merely a remembered-state claim.**

# 14. Conditional Theorem 3 — Noetic Divergence

If:
1. participants report comparable noetic intensity;
2. prior ontology, context, or integration frame differs;
3. later metaphysical interpretations diverge;

then:
> **Noetic quality is insufficient to determine metaphysical content.**

# 15. Conditional Theorem 4 — Predictive-Precision Mediation

If:
1. a validated precision-related variable changes acutely;
2. the change precedes altered salience or belief flexibility;
3. controlling for context does not eliminate the relation;
4. manipulating the predictive process changes the downstream effect;

then:
> **Predictive-precision change may be licensed as a mediating mechanism for selected PRF transformations.**

Claim ceiling:
> **Mediation ≠ complete mechanism.**

# 16. Conditional Theorem 5 — Boundary Decomposition

If self/world-boundary measures decompose reliably and vary independently, then:

> **Ego dissolution should be treated as a family of partially dissociable transformations rather than one unitary variable.**

# 17. Conditional Theorem 6 — Transfer Specificity

If acute effects are strong, near transfer is present, far transfer is weak, and delayed effects decay, then:

> **Acute transcendence can be psychologically profound without constituting generalized durable capability transformation.**

# 18. Conditional Theorem 7 — Context Vulnerability

If belief rigidity falls, suggestibility or authority dependence rises, and later belief change tracks guide-supplied interpretations, then:

> **The same openness that enables therapeutic revision may create a vulnerability channel for externally shaped belief installation.**

# 19. Corollary 1 — Transcendence Is Typed

> **Transcendence is a family of transformations, not a single scalar state.**

Types:
    PHENOMENAL
    SELF_MODEL
    TEMPORAL
    SOCIAL_INTERPERSONAL
    ONTOLOGICAL
    NORMATIVE
    AUTHORIAL

# 20. Corollary 2 — Benefit and Truth Can Diverge

> **Therapeutic success and metaphysical truth must be evaluated on separate tracks.**

# 21. Corollary 3 — Harm and Falsity Can Diverge

> **Distress does not imply falsity, and comfort does not imply truth.**

# 22. Corollary 4 — Non-Psychedelic Regression Is Required

If the architecture is genuinely about transcendence rather than psychedelic pharmacology, it must also survive:

- religious ritual;
- music-induced self-transcendence;
