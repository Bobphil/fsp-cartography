Meditations on Becoming, Understanding, and Human Possibility

A developing philosophical project in conversation with Augustine’s Confessions, Descartes’ Meditations and philosophy as a way of life. The illustrated prospectus explores scientific self-understanding, situated individuality, intellectual assembly and the responsible development of human possibilities.

The prospectus is the preserved conversational proposal, not a completed book or a report of human participant findings.

Invitation to readers

There is no prerequisite knowledge of FSP. Read at your own pace and consider the questions that matter to you. You need not adopt the author’s philosophical commitments.

  1. Does the Here I Am entrance invite your own philosophical inquiry?
  2. Are the comparisons with Augustine and Descartes illuminating?
  3. How might contemporary science enrich or challenge these meditations?
  4. Which of the twelve proposed meditations would you most like to explore?
  5. Which perspectives, objections or territories are missing?

Return observations directly to the person who invited you. No response form or automatic collection is provided. Please distinguish material you wish to keep private from comments you explicitly permit to be shared publicly.
