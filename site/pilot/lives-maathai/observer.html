Observer / Researcher View

Neutral recruitment message

I’m testing a new browser-based historical learning interface. I’m looking for people who have not used Full-Stack Philosophy before. The test is about whether the interface communicates clearly, not about your knowledge or performance. You’ll explore a short historical case and then answer seven questions about what you thought the page meant. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time.

Participant introduction / consent script

Thank you for helping test this interface. We are testing the interface, not you. Please use it in your normal way. I will not explain unfamiliar terms while you are exploring, because we need to know what the interface communicates by itself. If something is confusing, please say so. You may stop at any time. I will record your answers about the interface and brief usability notes; please do not share personal information that is not needed for the test.

For formal institutional or publishable human-subject research, confirm the applicable IRB/human-subject requirements before treating these observations as research data.

Frozen pass thresholds

A distinction passes if at least 4 of 5 readers can explain it in ordinary language without being supplied the FSP term.

  • At least 5 of 7 distinctions must pass overall.
  • Timeline period must pass.
  • Hidden future must pass.
  • Source vs. statement may collapse in no more than one reader.
  • Portability may become direct imitation in no more than one reader.
  • Return may become a single-correct-answer task in no more than one reader.
