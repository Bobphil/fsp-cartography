## Neutral recruitment message

I'm testing a new browser-based historical learning interface. I'm looking for people who have not used Full-Stack Philosophy before. The test is about whether the interface communicates clearly, not about your knowledge or performance. You'll explore a short historical case and then answer seven questions about what you thought the page meant. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time.

## Participant introduction

Thank you for helping test this interface. We are testing the interface, not you. Please use it in your normal way. I will not explain unfamiliar terms while you are exploring, because we need to know what the interface communicates by itself. If something is confusing, please say so. You may stop at any time.

## Privacy / consent boundary

For this formative pilot:
- use participant IDs rather than names;
- do not collect unnecessary personal information;
- participant page records no behavior automatically;
- observer notes and scores stay in local browser storage until exported;
- participants may stop at any time.

If results will be used as formal institutional or publishable human-subject research, confirm the applicable IRB / human-subject requirements before treating observations as research data.

## Frozen thresholds

A distinction passes if at least 4 of 5 participants can explain it in ordinary language without being supplied the FSP term.

Global baseline requires:
- at least 5 of 7 distinctions pass;
- timeline period passes;
- hidden future passes;
- source vs statement collapses in no more than one participant;
- portability becomes imitation in no more than one participant;
- Return becomes a single-correct-answer task in no more than one participant.

## Freeze rule

Do not change participant-facing wording during participants 1-5.

If a material usability defect requires a patch:
1. stop the cohort;
2. record the defect;
3. increment the baseline version;
4. restart the baseline cohort.

## What the first baseline is testing

Can a framework-naive reader distinguish:
1. bounded timeline period;
2. supported statement;
3. source;
4. capability change;
5. hidden future;
6. portable pattern without imitation;
7. learner-authored Return?

The test is not measuring recall of FSP vocabulary.
