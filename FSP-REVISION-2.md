# FSP-REVISION-2

## Revision-Reason Grammar, Evidence/Coherence/Simplicity/Fecundity Tradeoff Matrix, Good-Sense Comparator, Path-Dependence Audit, and Non-Algorithmic Choice Boundary

**Status:** Revision-reason governance layer  
**Boundary:** REVISION-1 remains intact; TRANSLATION-12 human evidence remains PENDING.

## 1. Purpose

REVISION-1 identified what can be revised.

REVISION-2 asks:

> What kinds of reasons can legitimately bear on choosing among live revision candidates when those reasons conflict?

The build does not create a theory-choice score.

It represents reasons separately and makes tradeoffs inspectable.

## 2. Revision reasons

The first grammar distinguishes:

- EVIDENCE_FIT
- COHERENCE
- SIMPLICITY
- FECUNDITY
- SCOPE
- CONSERVATISM
- EXPLANATORY_POWER

These correspond to familiar scientific-theory virtues and Quinean pressures such as minimal disturbance, while remaining represented as distinct considerations.

## 3. Tradeoff matrix

Each candidate receives one assessment per reason:

- STRONGLY_FAVORS
- FAVORS
- NEUTRAL
- COUNTS_AGAINST
- STRONGLY_COUNTS_AGAINST
- UNRESOLVED

The matrix can identify strict Pareto-style dominance when one candidate is no worse on every represented reason and better on at least one.

But it does not collapse reasons into a weighted sum.

Therefore many candidate pairs remain non-dominated.

That is a feature, not a failure.

## 4. Neptune, Vulcan, and relativity

The Neptune repair scores strongly on evidence fit and conservatism.

The Vulcan repair scores strongly on conservatism but badly on evidence fit and fecundity.

The relativity-style framework revision scores strongly on evidence fit, scope, fecundity, and explanatory power while counting against conservatism and local simplicity.

The case therefore exhibits genuine value conflict.

No single virtue orders all three candidates.

## 5. Good sense

Duhemian good sense is modeled as an evaluator profile that makes some reasons more salient than others without turning those priorities into a universal algorithm.

A cautious empiricist may place evidence fit and explanatory power first.

A conservative researcher may initially place conservatism and simplicity first.

Both can inspect the same candidate profile while reaching different provisional judgments.

The system records that difference.

It does not declare one evaluator uniquely rational merely because of the priority order.

## 6. Path dependence

Revision choice occurs inside inherited research practices.

The path-dependence audit records:

- inherited framework;
- normal practice;
- sunk infrastructure;
- training commitments;
- institutional constraints.

These may explain why one revision is easier, cheaper, more familiar, or institutionally available.

But:

> formation != justification.

Historical inheritance is not automatically epistemic support.

## 7. Formation/justification firewall

The audit separates:

- epistemically relevant path-dependent features;
- merely historical or institutional features.

For example, established computational techniques may matter evidentially if they improve measurement reliability.

By contrast, researcher familiarity alone may explain uptake without justifying a theory.

## 8. Non-algorithmic choice boundary

FSP may compare:

- evidence fit;
- coherence;
- simplicity;
- fecundity;
- scope;
- conservatism;
- explanatory power;
- path-dependent costs and affordances.

FSP may not compute:

- a universal weighted sum;
- a hidden recommendation score;
- historical familiarity as epistemic authority;
- a fixed lexical priority among theory virtues.

The final recommendation remains null.

## 9. Philosophical significance

REVISION-2 turns theory-choice virtues into an inspectable plural reason space.

Its contribution is not:

> here is the correct weighting of scientific virtues.

It is:

> here are the distinct reasons currently bearing on the revision frontier, where they conflict, how path dependence shapes availability and uptake, and where rational disagreement remains.

## 10. Human-validation boundary

Nothing in REVISION-2 changes the frozen TRANSLATION-12 human-evidence state.

Human validation remains PENDING.

## 11. Research ceiling

The strongest claim is:

> FSP can represent conflicting revision reasons and path-dependent pressures without collapsing them into a single theory-choice score, while preserving the distinction between historically formed preference and epistemic justification.

It does not establish a universal account of scientific rationality.
