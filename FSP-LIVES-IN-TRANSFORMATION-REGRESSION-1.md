# FSP-LIVES-IN-TRANSFORMATION-REGRESSION-1

## Aristotle / Mandela Two-Case Portability Regression Against the Maathai Pilot

**Status:** THREE-TOPOLOGY REGRESSION — VERSION 1  
**Parents:** FSP-LIVES-IN-TRANSFORMATION-PILOT-1; FSP-LIVES-IN-TRANSFORMATION-ARISTOTLE-1; FSP-LIVES-IN-TRANSFORMATION-MANDELA-1  
**Primary purpose:** Determine whether the Lives-in-Transformation grammar is genuinely portable across distinct historical topologies or whether it is secretly a Maathai-shaped biography template.  
**Decision rule:** Shared fields may remain only if they preserve rather than erase domain-specific differences.

---

# 1. Three Dominant Topologies

## Maathai

**bounded practical task → repeated coordination → capability expansion → institutional friction → widened problem map → civic / political action → failed electoral attempt → later institutional re-location**

## Aristotle

**institutional formation → relocation → widening empirical / philosophical inquiry → court-connected interval → research-institution creation → text / method differentiation → political displacement**

## Mandela

**mass political organization → nonviolent resistance → repression / banning → armed-struggle revision → imprisonment → negotiation under constraint → restored collective leadership → democratic election → state authority**

These are not variants of one story.

---

# 2. Shared Grammar Test

The following fields remain useful across all three:

- situated state;
- capability;
- constraint;
- relationships / institutions;
- live possibilities;
- transition;
- consequence;
- re-location;
- source ledger;
- claim ceiling;
- Return;
- portability.

**PASS.**

---

# 3. Optional-Field Test

The following cannot be mandatory in identical form:

- intended transformation;
- motive reconstruction;
- failed plan;
- explicit strategy;
- self-map;
- text-level concept development.

Aristotle demonstrates why.

Some fields are source-poor or category-inappropriate.

**PASS — OPTIONALITY REQUIRED.**

---

# 4. Evidence-Density Test

## Maathai

Mixed first-person, institutional, NGO, human-rights, and later historical sources.

## Aristotle

Late ancient biography + modern scholarly synthesis + rich surviving texts; motive evidence weak.

## Mandela

Dense first-person speeches, institutional archives, timelines, retrospective accounts, and government records.

If the public reader presents these as equally certain, the architecture fails.

**PASS — EVIDENCE-DENSITY MUST BE VISIBLE.**

---

# 5. Hindsight-Control Test

## Maathai

Do not import Nobel Prize / 2002 victory into 1977.

## Aristotle

Do not import later canonical status or Alexander's conquests into Academy / tutoring states.

## Mandela

Do not import presidency or successful negotiation into 1952 / 1961 / 1985 states.

**PASS.**

---

# 6. Capability-Trajectory Test

## Maathai

Grassroots organizational capability expands and later converts partly into institutional standing.

## Aristotle

Inquiry capability becomes institutionalized in the Lyceum; Macedonian ties shift from resource to vulnerability.

## Mandela

Direct physical capability contracts drastically under imprisonment while symbolic / political standing persists or expands; formal authority arrives only after democratic election.

**PASS.**

This strongly supports the claim:

> **Capability is vector-like and context-relative.**

---

# 7. Failed-Plan Test

## Maathai

Clear failed electoral plan in 1997.

## Aristotle

No well-sourced failed plan should be forced into the dossier.

## Mandela

Better represented through strategy revision under changing constraints than one discrete failed plan.

**PASS — FAILED PLAN IS NOT A UNIVERSAL REQUIRED FIELD.**

---

# 8. Intended / Actual Test

## Maathai

Strong enough evidence for several bounded intended / actual contrasts.

## Aristotle

Often too weak at the personal-motive level; text-level and institutional actions are safer.

## Mandela

Strong first-person and institutional evidence supports explicit strategy reconstruction in several periods.

**PASS — INTENT MUST BE EVIDENCE-TYPED.**

---

# 9. Institution Test

## Maathai

Institutions appear as constraints, organizing contexts, and later formal office.

## Aristotle

Institution itself becomes an inquiry capability: Academy and Lyceum.

## Mandela

Institutions are coercive, oppositional, organizational, negotiating, constitutional, and executive.

**PASS.**

No single definition of "institutional role" is sufficient without typed relation.

---

# 10. Authorization Test

## Maathai

Distinguish public influence from later elected / ministerial authority.

## Aristotle

Authorization is less central; institutional membership and founding matter more than political authority.

## Mandela

Authorization is central: ANC role, MK command, prison talks before full consultation, formal negotiation, democratic presidency.

**PASS — AUTHORIZATION IS SALIENT BY CASE, NOT FORCED UNIFORMLY.**

---

# 11. Concept-Development Test

Aristotle requires a field unavailable in the same way for Maathai and Mandela:

**TEXTUAL / CONCEPTUAL TRANSFORMATION**.

The field must remain separate from inferred personal motive.

**PASS — DOMAIN-LOCAL DECOMPRESSION, NOT NEW CORE PRIMITIVE.**

---

# 12. Person / Dossier Firewall

All three retain:

> **Biographical dossier ≠ person.**

But the reason differs:

- Maathai: retrospective and institutional narratives remain partial;
- Aristotle: ancient biographical evidence is sparse and late;
- Mandela: dense records still do not equal total first-person access.

**PASS.**

---

# 13. Exemplar-Portability Test

## Maathai

Portable candidate: bounded task may build capability and reveal wider constraints.

## Aristotle

Portable candidate: inquiry institutions can expand tractable problem space; formation need not equal intellectual stasis.

## Mandela

Portable candidate: strategy may need revision when constraints and authorization structures change.

None becomes a recommendation.

**PASS.**

---

# 14. Maathai-Template Ablation

Remove these Maathai-specific expectations:

- grassroots practical task;
- community organizing;
- environmental problem;
- electoral failure as mandatory;
- movement-to-office sequence.

Can Aristotle and Mandela still be represented?

**YES.**

Therefore the shared grammar is not dependent on the Maathai narrative.

---

# 15. Shared-Core Ablation

Remove:

- state;
- transition;
- capability;
- constraint;
- source ledger;
- claim ceiling;
- Return.

Can the three dossiers still be compared without losing important structure?

**NO.**

These remain justified shared research fields.

---

# 16. Regression Decision

**PASS — LIVES-IN-TRANSFORMATION GRAMMAR IS PORTABLE ACROSS THREE DISTINCT TOPOLOGIES.**

Retain shared grammar.

Modify implementation rules:

1. fields may be optional when evidence or topology does not support them;
2. evidence density must be visible;
3. motive and intent must be source-typed;
4. concept-development traces must remain distinct from life-motive reconstruction;
5. authorization is case-salient, not universally dominant;
6. failed plan is optional;
7. domain-local fields may decompress without entering Core.

---

# 17. Encyclopedia Implication

The public Lives route should not present one fixed card stack with every field equally populated.

Use:

**common shell + topology-sensitive modules**

Common shell:

**PERSON → STATES → TRANSITIONS → SOURCES → CAPABILITY / CONSTRAINT → RETURN → PORTABILITY**

Optional modules:

- intended / actual;
- failed plan;
- strategy;
- authorization;
- text / concept development;
- institutional role;
- counterfactual probe.

---

# 18. VR Implication

Maathai remains the first VR pilot because her transition is well suited to experiential capability growth.

But regression requires future VR projection profiles to vary.

## Aristotle VR

Research environment / inquiry institution / evidence and method, not simulated private mind.

## Mandela VR

Changing power, legitimacy, legal status, organizational authorization, and negotiation constraints.

Do not reuse the Maathai scene grammar mechanically.

---

# 19. Claim Ceiling

This regression establishes portability of the dossier grammar across three deliberately different cases.

It does not establish:

- universal applicability to all lives;
- historical completeness of any dossier;
- educational effectiveness;
- VR effectiveness;
- that Aristotle, Mandela, and Maathai exhaust relevant topology classes.

---

# 20. REOPEN Conditions

Reopen if:

1. a dialogical / artistic / religious life cannot fit without distortion;
2. evidence-poor ancient lives require too much inference;
3. non-Western or collective-agent histories expose hidden assumptions;
4. public readers interpret missing fields as missing life features rather than source limits;
5. topology-sensitive modules proliferate into a second ontology.

---

# 21. Constitutional Decision

**PASS — RETAIN COMMON SHELL + TOPOLOGY-SENSITIVE MODULES.**

No new Core primitive.

The next execution move may now safely be:

**public Lives-route specification + Maathai VR projection specification**, while Aristotle and Mandela remain regression dossiers for topology and source-governance testing.
