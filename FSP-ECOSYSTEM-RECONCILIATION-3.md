FSP's research architecture has advanced faster than its public and classroom projections.

The ecosystem now contains mature or near-mature work on constitutional compression, Four-Move Governance, Simon / Brandom / Price / Ismael comparators, situated agency, Transformation Architecture, Objective Phenomenology, heterogeneous intelligence, ontological interoperability, recursive scale, capability and reachability, personal life navigation, Lives in Transformation, Completion Horizon, and VR 2.0.

These developments are not yet uniformly visible in the public Encyclopedia research surface, Pressbook narratives, classroom workbooks, public case routes, or VR implementation.

The required move is therefore not conceptual expansion.

It is controlled reconciliation.

# 6. Research Region Rule

A research region is a navigation and orientation layer.

It does not become a second owner of canonical concepts, case dossiers, source ledgers, research builds, or claim ceilings.

Each region must route to existing owners.

# 7. Research Orientation Essay Pattern

Each research region should have one public orientation essay with the following structure:

1. Why this problem matters.
2. What maps currently exist.
3. Where translation burdens arise.
4. What FSP currently contributes.
5. What does not yet follow.
6. What empirical / philosophical work remains.
7. Where to go deeper.

The orientation essay must remain readable as prose.

It should not read like a schema dump.

# 9. Agency Research Region

Recommended entrance question:

> **What kind of agency do real situated agents possess, under what conditions is it enlarged or impaired, and what follows ethically from the different ways action is caused, constrained, enabled, and transformed?**

- Free Will / Determinism;
- Situated Agency;
- Bounded Rationality;
- Capability;
- Neuroethics;
- Distributed Agency;
- Institutional Agency;
- Transformation;
- Responsibility.

- causal explanation ≠ ethical verdict;
- impairment ≠ complete absence of agency;
- capability ≠ authorization;
- distributed causation ≠ absence of responsibility.

# 39. VR Pilot — Required Scenes

## Scene 1 — Situated Entry

Expose place, task, institutional constraints, known relationships, and evidence available then.

Hide later fame, later outcome, and future interpretation.

## Scene 2 — Possibility Field

Learner inspects live possibilities, constraints, resources, capability, and affected parties.

## Scene 3 — Transformation Probe

Learner identifies intended change, assumptions, strategy, and expected consequence.

## Scene 4 — Historical Reveal

Reveal documented action, consequence, source provenance, and claim ceiling.

## Scene 5 — Re-location

Show capability delta, relationship delta, institutional change, opened / closed futures, and remainder.

## Scene 6 — Portability Gate

Ask what structural relation might travel, what must not travel, and what differs in the learner's context.

## Scene 7 — Authorial Return

Generate no recommendation.

Return bounded insight, unresolved remainder, and optional Life Navigation handoff.

## Scene 8 — OFF

End outside the immersive scaffold.

# 43. VR Claim-Ceiling Display

# 44. VR Remainder Representation

Remainder should be experienceable as unresolved edge, absent source, disputed interpretation, inaccessible perspective, unverified motive, or unknown consequence.

Do not render remainder as visual mystery for spectacle.
