# FSP-ENCYCLOPEDIA-DOMAIN-NAV-1

## Multi-domain public navigation and domain landing architecture

### Public hierarchy

The universal Encyclopedia navigation is now defined as:

```text
FSP Encyclopedia
├── Domains
├── Core
├── Resources
├── Routes
└── About
```

Domain-native navigation lives below that level.

For Inquiry & Science:

```text
Inquiry & Science
├── Chapters
├── Cases
├── Concepts
├── Tools
└── Comparisons
```

This preserves every existing working science route without allowing the science collection to define the universal Encyclopedia shell.

### Reader-facing status

Internal production codes remain authoritative in the data model, but the public shell translates them:

- ACTIVE-MATURE → Available now
- SUBSTANTIAL-INTEGRATION-PENDING → In development
- SCOPED → Planned
- CROSS-DOMAIN → Cross-domain resource
- FOUNDATIONAL → Foundation

### Functional domain pages

**Inquiry & Science** retains all current public Encyclopedia resources.

**History of Philosophy** exposes the existing *What May We Carry Forward?* Pressbook and its SUPER inquiry workspace. This does not claim that the whole Pressbook has already been ingested into the Encyclopedia.

**Ethics** exposes the certified SUPER workbook environment and Power & Flourishing Laboratory. No Ethics Pressbooks URL is exposed until a current public binding is explicitly verified.

**Logic & Argument**, **Aesthetics**, and **Institutions & Connected Doing** receive real landing pages with honest development status and no fabricated resources.

### Home-page reconciliation

The universal Home page should introduce domains and routes first.

The detailed five-case scientific program belongs inside the Inquiry & Science domain page, not beneath every domain on the universal Home page.

### Governance

1. Universal navigation is domain-neutral.
2. Domain-native navigation may differ by field.
3. Existing mature routes are preserved.
4. No dead or predicted public links are presented as available resources.
5. Internal maturity and public-facing status are distinct representations of the same production state.
6. A domain landing page may be useful before full Encyclopedia ingestion by linking to certified external canonical/course resources.
7. Science remains the first mature domain, not the universal navigation grammar.
