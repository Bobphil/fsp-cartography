# FSP-PRF-VR-3.1

## Experiential-Integrator Reconciliation, Assembly-History Reintegration, Cognitive-Prosthesis Lock, OFF/Return Transfer Architecture, and Heterogeneous-Sensory Extension

**Status:** EXPERIENTIAL-INTEGRATOR RECONCILIATION — VERSION 3.1  
**Role:** Reconcile the earlier FSP-VR Experiential Integrator architecture with PRF-VR-3 so that heterogeneous sensory worlds, Objective Phenomenology, Joint Inquiry, and virtual-to-physical experiment design remain bounded extensions inside the larger constitutional purpose of FSP-VR.  
**Primary purpose:** Restore assembly history, cognitive decompression, the unrepresented center, OFF, Authorial Return, and transformed post-scaffold capability as first-class constraints while preserving the newer sensory-access and experimental machinery.  
**Primary claim ceiling:** FSP-VR is not a total simulation of persons, organisms, institutions, or worlds. It is a cognitive prosthesis that temporarily decomposes relations so users can inspect them, then returns authorship and judgment to lived Doing.

---

# 1. Reconciliation Problem

PRF-VR-3 significantly expanded FSP-VR by adding:

- heterogeneous sensory profiles;
- detectable / discriminable / actionable distinctions;
- Umwelt Constraint Simulation;
- Phenomenal Translation Remainder;
- multi-agent Joint Inquiry;
- virtual-to-physical experiment design;
- Pattern of Discovery transfer testing.

These additions are valuable.

But taken alone they could narrow FSP-VR into:

- a sensory simulation platform;
- an animal-consciousness laboratory;
- an AI/agent comparison environment;
- a scientific experiment-design tool.

The earlier FSP-VR architecture had a broader constitutional role:

> **FSP-VR is the experiential integrator of the FSP ecosystem.**

It makes relations among histories, persons, institutions, evidence, models, possibilities, consequences, and Return experienceable without replacing the artifacts that own those domains.

PRF-VR-3.1 restores that larger role.

---

# 2. Constitutional Lock

FSP-VR remains:

> **a cognitive prosthesis, not a philosophical machine.**

It must not:

- replace Pressbooks;
- replace SUPER;
- replace domain laboratories;
- infer a person's true mind;
- provide final philosophical conclusions;
- become the canonical owner of domain content.

Its distinctive function is:

> **to make relationships among bounded representations perceptually, temporally, and practically inspectable.**

# 7. Current Access ≠ Complete Access Explanation

The architecture must preserve:

> **Current sensory profile ≠ complete explanation of current actionable world.**

For example:

- learning can alter discrimination;
- prior injury can alter compensation;
- institutional training can alter salience;
- developmental history can alter sensorimotor coupling;
- prior regeneration can alter biological capacity;
- model training can alter artificial feature access.

Thus:

> **Present access ≠ history-free access.**

# 10. Person / Map Geometry

The earlier Experiential Integrator established a constitutional geometry:

> each person inhabits one Doing first-personally while others are encountered through bounded maps.

PRF-VR-3.1 retains that.

A represented person may include:

- direct report;
- testimony;
- action;
- institutional records;
- artifacts;
- measurements;
- inferred context;
- later interpretation.

But no representation becomes the person.

> **My map of another person ≠ the other person.**

---

# 11. The Unrepresented Center

Every represented person or phenomenal subject should preserve an **unrepresented center**.

This is not an empty placeholder for mystical content.

It marks the constitutional limit:

> the system does not possess the person's first-person Doing.

The same principle generalizes carefully to nonhuman phenomenality:

> **Constraint map ≠ possessed phenomenal world.**

---

PRF-VR-3's Phenomenal Translation Remainder is therefore not a new philosophical idea unrelated to earlier FSP-VR.

It is a formal extension of the prior rule:

> **There should always remain a region we do not possess.**

For human persons:

```text
bounded evidence
→ person-map
→ unrepresented first-person center
```

For nonhuman objective phenomenology:

```text
cross-species evidence
→ Umwelt Constraint Map
→ phenomenal translation remainder
```

# 22. There I Am / Yes—Here I Am / I See Me

The earlier architecture distinguished three forms of recognition.

## There I am

Local recognition:

> a bounded representation fits well enough for the user to claim it.

## Yes—here I am

Architectural recognition:

> the user recognizes themselves as the first-person center from which this inquiry is being instantiated.

## I see me

Diachronic recognition:

> across revisions, commitments, consequences, abandoned paths, and reopened questions, the person recognizes an unfolding trajectory as their own.

The system must never assert the final recognition for the user.

> **System evidence for recognition ≠ user self-recognition.**

---

# 23. Diachronic Replay

- prior maps;
- prior commitments;
- changed salience;
- changed capability;
- consequences;
- abandoned routes;
- reopened judgments.

Replay becomes assembly-history inspection.

# 58. Strongest Supported Claim

The strongest licensed architectural claim is:

> **FSP-VR can serve as an experiential integrator by temporarily decompressing historically assembled, perspectivally bounded, and heterogeneously accessed relations; making their provenance, limits, and remainders inspectable; and then fading the scaffold so any durable gain must survive the user's return to lived Doing.**

This is stronger than calling VR a visualization tool.

It remains weaker than claiming transformed perception has already been demonstrated.

---

# 59. Claim Ceiling

PRF-VR-3.1 does not establish:

- that VR produces durable cognitive transformation;
- that users gain another person's or organism's first-person perspective;
- that all access differences can be simulated;
- that assembly history is fully recoverable;
- that institutional filters are literally sensory systems;
- that an unrepresented center proves a particular metaphysics of self;
- that VR can replace historical scholarship;
- that experimental productivity justifies intervention;
- that the system can determine who the user truly is.

---

# 60. REOPEN Conditions

Reopen if:

- sensory extensions begin to dominate the larger Experiential Integrator role;
- users confuse represented persons with modeled profiles;
- OFF-state transfer consistently fails;
- assembly-history visualization encourages deterministic narratives;
- the unrepresented-center metaphor becomes misleading;
- domain-native laboratories lose their own topology;
- the cognitive-prosthesis metaphor no longer fits actual use;
- users retain terminology without changed discrimination;
- Return Cards become formulaic rather than authorial;
- new empirical work requires a different transfer model.
