## Interaction grammar

The bridge defines these primitives:

- INSPECT
- MOVE_VIEWPOINT
- TOGGLE_PROBE
- REPLAY_TRANSITION
- COMPARE
- OVERVIEW
- RETURN

The first scene uses INSPECT, TOGGLE_PROBE, REPLAY_TRANSITION, COMPARE, and OVERVIEW.

Replay may change tempo and viewpoint only. It may not alter canonical sequence, provenance, or claim strength.

## Viewpoint firewall

Viewpoint changes perceptual availability.

It does not change the truth of the canonical object.

Perspective ≠ person.

Spatial relation ≠ semantic relation.

## Scaffold fading

Certified sequence:

1. Guided
2. Reduced
3. Independent

Fading removes assistance rather than canonical constraints.

Persistent simulation status and return constraints survive scaffold removal.

## Debrief

VR completion requires exit from immersion into a NON_IMMERSIVE debrief.

Required fields:

- experienced change;
- licensed inference;
- unlicensed inference;
- remaining uncertainty;
- REOPEN condition.

The scene cannot be marked complete before the debrief.

## Return contract

The participant, not the scene, states the final bounded judgment.

Immersive salience may not increase evidential standing or claim strength.

## Claim ceiling

This certification establishes a concrete VR interaction grammar and one scene specification.

It does not establish that VR improves comprehension, transfer, retention, or judgment. Those remain research questions.
