# FSP-ENCYCLOPEDIA-AGENCY-CAPACITY-1

## Agency/Capacity/Responsibility Cluster, Scale-of-Explanation Firewalls, Personal-Continuity Concept, Manifest/Scientific Image Translation Links, and Regression Test

**Status:** ENCYCLOPEDIA CANONICAL-SUPPORT EXTENSION — VERSION 1  
**Role:** Add a bounded set of stable distinctions that strengthen the current Encyclopedia Core without promoting unresolved Research Frontiers into canonical doctrine.  
**Primary scope:** agency, capacity, responsibility, explanatory scale, personal continuity, and translation between lived/manifest and scientific descriptions.  
**Primary claim ceiling:** This build clarifies relationships among already established FSP distinctions. It does not establish a final theory of agency, responsibility, personal identity, reduction, emergence, consciousness, or moral standing.

---

# 1. Why this build is needed

Recent work on neurological impairment, multiscale agency, biosemiotics, enhanced humans, synthetic and hybrid systems, and normative profiles exposed several recurring ambiguities.

The Encyclopedia already distinguishes:

- map from world;
- evidence from warrant;
- agency from moral standing;
- recommendation from authorization;
- translation from identity;
- current judgment from final closure.

But four related areas still need a clearer canonical home:

1. agency / capacity / responsibility;
2. scale of explanation;
3. personal continuity;
4. translation between manifest/lived and scientific descriptions.

These are stable enough to support current Encyclopedia routing now.

They should not be confused with the still-open Research Frontiers:

- **Pattern of Discovery (RF-01)**;
- **Cross-Perspectival Calibration (RF-02)**;
- **Agency-Substrate Extraction (RF-03)**;
- **Temporal Interoperability (RF-04)**;
- **Repair Topology (RF-05)**.

> **Canonical clarification ≠ Frontier promotion.**

---

# 2. Agency / Capacity / Responsibility Cluster

The Encyclopedia should no longer treat "agency" or "responsibility" as single undifferentiated properties.

```text
AGENCY
├─ capability
├─ control
├─ reason-responsiveness
├─ competence
├─ constraint
├─ responsibility
├─ blame
├─ protection
├─ treatment / support
├─ standing
└─ authorization
```

These terms are related. They are not interchangeable.

---

# 3. Agency

**Agency** concerns what a system can do within a task space under relevant constraints.

Agency can vary by scale, domain, task, embodiment, history, environmental support, internal organization, and available information.

> **Agency ≠ unlimited freedom.**

> **Agency ≠ moral standing.**

The second distinction remains governed by the Normative Profile architecture.

---

# 4. Capacity

**Capacity** refers to an ability relevant to a task or judgment.

Examples include understanding, inhibition, planning, memory, attention, communication, recognizing reasons, acting on reasons, and recovering from error.

A person may retain one capacity while losing another.

> **Capacity ≠ all-or-nothing agency.**

> **Impairment in one capacity ≠ absence of all agency.**

---

# 5. Control

**Control** concerns the degree to which an agent can regulate action relative to relevant goals, reasons, or constraints.

Control can be intact, partial, unstable, context-sensitive, externally constrained, or internally impaired.

> **Causal involvement ≠ control.**

> **Control ≠ ultimate causal authorship.**

This allows FSP to discuss responsibility without first solving the metaphysical free-will debate.

---

# 6. Reason-responsiveness

**Reason-responsiveness** concerns whether an agent can recognize reasons, distinguish relevant considerations, revise in response to reasons, inhibit action when reasons count against it, and sustain action when reasons support it.

An agent may know a rule yet lack ordinary capacity to regulate behavior in accordance with it.

> **Knowing a reason ≠ being able to act on that reason.**

---

# 7. Competence

**Competence** is always competence for something.

Medical competence, legal competence, task competence, moral reason-responsiveness, and practical implementation competence need not coincide.

> **Competence in one domain ≠ competence in all domains.**

Competence judgments should state task, threshold, evidence, observer, decision consequence, and claim ceiling.

---

# 8. Constraint

Constraint may arise from physical condition, neurological impairment, coercion, institutional structure, poverty, information deficit, time pressure, social dependence, technological architecture, or environmental design.

Constraint does not automatically eliminate agency.

But it can narrow responsibility.

> **Remaining agency ≠ unlimited responsibility.**

---

# 9. Responsibility

Responsibility should be treated as a structured judgment rather than a direct synonym for causation.

A responsibility assessment may ask:

```text
What happened?
What capacities were relevant?
What did the agent understand?
What control was available?
What alternatives were realistically available?
What reasons could the agent recognize?
What constraints operated?
What role did prior choices play?
What role did institutional structure play?
Who else had authority or control?
What harms followed?
```

FSP does not convert this into a universal responsibility score.

---

# 10. Responsibility Firewalls

> **Causal explanation ≠ moral excuse.**

> **Causal contribution ≠ full responsibility.**

> **Remaining agency ≠ unlimited responsibility.**

> **Responsibility ≠ blame.**

> **Blame ≠ protection.**

> **Reduced culpability ≠ reduced standing of affected others.**

> **Treatment ≠ protection.**

> **Protection ≠ punishment.**

These distinctions allow compassion, accountability, protection, treatment, and justice to remain separately inspectable.

---

# 11. Standing remains separate

This build does not derive moral standing from agency, capacity, competence, or reason-responsiveness.

The current Normative Profile architecture remains authoritative for standing.

> **Agency profile ≠ standing profile.**

> **Capacity profile ≠ moral-worth ranking.**

> **Impaired competence ≠ diminished human moral standing under NP-HUMAN-RECIPROCITY.**

---

# 12. Authorization remains separate

An agent's capacity does not by itself determine who may intervene.

Intervention requires separate attention to standing, legal authority, institutional authority, consent, risk, proportionality, alternatives, reversibility, and review.

> **Need for help ≠ authorization to override agency.**

> **Impairment ≠ unlimited intervention authority.**

---

# 13. Scale-of-Explanation Cluster

Modern inquiry often describes the same system at multiple scales:

```text
molecular
cellular
tissue
organism
person
relationship
institution
population
ecosystem
```

Different scales may answer different questions.

A local mechanism can be real. A higher-order pattern can also be real.

Neither automatically excludes the other.

---

# 14. Scale-of-Explanation Firewalls

> **Scale of explanation ≠ exclusivity of explanation.**

> **Substrate explanation ≠ exhaustive explanation of organized competence.**

> **Higher-level pattern ≠ independence from substrate.**

> **Higher-level coordination ≠ composite phenomenal person.**

> **Formal resemblance across scales ≠ explanatory identity.**

> **Cross-domain analogy ≠ causal derivation.**

These constraints support multiscale inquiry without requiring reductive exclusivity or uncritical holism.

---

# 15. Explanatory aperture

FSP should treat scale as an **aperture choice**.

A useful question is:

> What becomes visible at this scale, and what becomes invisible?

For each explanatory aperture, record where useful:

- target phenomenon;
- scale;
- variables;
- mechanism;
- evidence;
- unresolved remainder;
- relation to adjacent scales;
- claim ceiling.

This provides a stable bridge to **Cross-Perspectival Calibration (RF-02)** without promoting that Frontier's stronger hypotheses.

---

# 16. Personal Continuity Concept

FSP needs a typed representation of continuity because "same person" can hide multiple questions.

```text
PersonalContinuity = {
  biologicalContinuity,
  bodilyContinuity,
  memoryContinuity,
  phenomenalContinuity,
  agencyContinuity,
  relationalContinuity,
  narrativeContinuity,
  legalContinuity,
  socialRoleContinuity,
  selfIdentification,
  contestedAspects,
  evidence,
  claimCeiling
}
```

This is not a metaphysical theory of personal identity.

It is an inspection framework for cases where different continuities diverge.

---

# 17. Personal Continuity Firewalls

> **Personal continuity ≠ unchanged agential capacity.**

> **Changed agency ≠ new person by default.**

> **Biological continuity ≠ complete personal identity criterion.**

> **Memory continuity ≠ complete personal identity criterion.**

> **Legal continuity ≠ unchanged phenomenal or agential continuity.**

> **Classification change ≠ standing change.**

---

# 18. Continuity and enhanced humans

The Personal Continuity concept supports the Levin / Diverse Agency extension.

A modified human may retain self-identification, relationships, memory, narrative, and legal identity while changing sensory capacities, morphology, cognition, biological substrate, or agency profile.

> **Modification ≠ category loss.**

> **Category loss ≠ moral-standing loss.**

These remain linked to the Normative Profile rather than derived from continuity alone.

---

# 19. Manifest and Scientific Image Translation

FSP should provide explicit links between lived / manifest descriptions and scientific descriptions.

A person may be described as grieving, afraid, responsible, exhausted, unable to focus, or feeling unlike themselves.

Scientific descriptions may refer to neural activity, hormonal state, lesion, pharmacological effect, statistical risk, network dynamics, or physiological constraint.

Both can matter.

Neither is automatically a replacement for the other.

---

# 20. Translation Firewalls: Manifest / Scientific Image

> **Scientific description ≠ replacement of lived description.**

> **Lived description ≠ exemption from scientific evidence.**

> **Phenomenal significance ≠ metaphysical proof.**

> **Therapeutic efficacy ≠ ontological validation.**

> **Scientific mechanism ≠ complete ethical judgment.**

> **Manifest vocabulary ≠ scientific error by default.**

Together these support **cooperation without convergence between explanatory vocabularies**.

---

# 21. Translation link structure

```text
MANIFEST / LIVED DESCRIPTION
↕
SCIENTIFIC DESCRIPTION
```

## Lived → scientific

- What scientific evidence bears on this experience?
- Which variables are measurable?
- What does the scientific model add?
- What does it leave out?

## Scientific → lived

- How does this mechanism alter actual capability?
- What does the affected person report?
- Which social roles or relationships change?
- What does the model not tell us about meaning or value?

This is a translation relation, not an identity relation.

---

# 22. Relationship to Cross-Perspectival Calibration (RF-02)

The present build does not settle the open Frontier question of how heterogeneous evidence perspectives can formally constrain one another.

```text
current Core:
typed evidence + translation + remainder + claim ceiling
↓
RF-02 — Cross-Perspectival Calibration:
how should different perspectives mutually constrain one another?
```

> **Translation link ≠ calibrated evidence fusion.**

---

# 23. Relationship to Pattern of Discovery (RF-01)

This build provides concepts that students or readers may learn to discriminate.

It does not establish that repeated use becomes a trained perceptual capacity.

That remains **Pattern of Discovery (RF-01)**.

> **Canonical Tool availability ≠ demonstrated perceptual transfer.**

---

# 24. Relationship to Agency-Substrate Extraction (RF-03)

The Agency / Capacity cluster makes it possible to distinguish ordinary constraint, impaired capacity, altered control, and changed reason-responsiveness.

It does not yet establish a canonical taxonomy for interventions that alter the substrate of agency.

That remains **Agency-Substrate Extraction (RF-03)**.

> **Capacity alteration ≠ established Agency-Substrate Extraction category.**

---

# 25. Relationship to Temporal Interoperability (RF-04)

Responsibility and competence judgments may depend on timing.

But this build does not alter the canonical timeline or state-transition architecture.

That remains **Temporal Interoperability (RF-04)**.

---

# 26. Relationship to Repair Topology (RF-05)

The distinction among blame, protection, treatment, and support pressures the idea of Repair.

But this build does not revise the JTS grammar.

The current canonical JTS layer remains:

> **REPAIR**

The question of whether repair should distinguish restoration, redesign, refusal, exit, parallel construction, counter-institution, or replacement remains **Repair Topology (RF-05)**.

# 28. Public-facing compression

## Agency / Responsibility

> What could this person actually understand, control, and do?

> What constrained them?

> What responsibility still follows?

> What protection or support is needed?

## Scale

> What does this explanation show at this level?

> What might another scale show that this one misses?

## Continuity

> What stayed continuous, and what changed?

## Manifest / Scientific Translation

> What does the scientific description add?

> What does the lived description preserve that the scientific model does not?

The Standard and Deep views may expose the formal distinctions and Firewalls.

> **Progressive disclosure ≠ semantic mutation.**

# 30. Regression invariants

The build passes only if all of the following remain true.

**R1 — Standing separation:** Agency, capacity, competence, and continuity do not determine moral standing automatically.

**R2 — Normative Profile ownership:** Substantive moral-standing rules remain owned by the Normative Profile architecture.

**R3 — Levin firewall preservation:** Detected agency does not become phenomenality or moral standing.

**R4 — Sebeok firewall preservation:** Semiosis does not become consciousness or standing.

**R5 — No free-will overclaim:** The responsibility cluster does not claim to solve determinism, libertarianism, compatibilism, or ultimate authorship.

**R6 — No reductive exclusivity:** Lower-level mechanism does not automatically erase higher-level explanation.

**R7 — No emergent reification:** Higher-level coordination does not automatically become a composite phenomenal person.

**R8 — Continuity neutrality:** Personal Continuity remains an inspection concept, not a final metaphysical identity theory.

**R9 — Manifest/scientific non-substitution:** Neither lived nor scientific description automatically replaces the other.

**R10 — Frontier firewall:** RF-01 through RF-05 remain Research Frontiers and are not promoted by this build.

**R11 — JTS stability:** The canonical JTS grammar remains unchanged.

**R12 — Public-reader stability:** No new top-level public navigation entrance is required.

---

# 31. Regression verdict criteria

The package is:

> **PASS — CANONICAL-SUPPORT EXTENSION**

if all twelve invariants hold, the cluster adds no hidden ontology, no Frontier concept is silently promoted, existing canonical ownership remains clear, and Quick / Standard / Deep compression preserves the same distinctions.

It is:

> **FAIL — ARCHITECTURAL OVERREACH**

if any new object assigns standing from agency automatically, treats scientific description as exhaustive, declares one scale uniquely real, defines a final theory of personal identity, changes JTS Repair, or promotes RF-01 through RF-05 without the Promotion-to-Core Gate.

---

# 32. Claim ceiling

This build supports:

> **FSP can represent agency, capacity, control, competence, responsibility, personal continuity, explanatory scale, and manifest/scientific translation as distinct but related objects without reducing them to one another.**

It does not establish:

- a universal theory of responsibility;
- a solution to free will;
- a complete theory of personal identity;
- a universal ontology of emergence;
- a final hierarchy of explanatory scales;
- a method for fusing first- and third-person evidence;
- a complete ethics of enhancement or impairment.

---

# 33. REOPEN conditions

Reopen this cluster if:

- cases show that capacity and control cannot be distinguished usefully;
- legal, medical, philosophical, or disability perspectives expose a missing responsibility dimension;
- Personal Continuity proves too coarse or too theory-laden;
- scale Firewalls suppress legitimate cross-scale explanation;
- manifest/scientific translation repeatedly fails in practice;
- Cross-Perspectival Calibration (RF-02) produces a stronger canonical evidence architecture;
- Agency-Substrate Extraction (RF-03) requires a revised capacity model;
- Temporal Interoperability (RF-04) shows timing must enter responsibility directly;
- Repair Topology (RF-05) changes how protection, treatment, or support are represented;
- empirical or conceptual work reveals hidden ableist, reductionist, or anthropocentric assumptions.

---

# 34. Final statement

This extension makes the Encyclopedia better able to ask:

> **What can this agent actually do, under these conditions, at this scale, and what follows ethically from that—without asking one description to do the work of all the others?**

It preserves:

```text
what the system can do
≠
what capacities are currently available
≠
what responsibility follows
≠
what moral standing the being possesses
```

It also preserves:

```text
one scale
≠
the only scale

scientific description
≠
the whole lived world

continuity
≠
unchangedness
```

These distinctions create a more stable platform for future Ethics, Inquiry, neuroethics, enhancement, Levin, Sebeok, and Research Frontier work without prematurely deciding questions that still deserve research.
