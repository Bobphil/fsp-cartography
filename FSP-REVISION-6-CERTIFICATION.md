# FSP-REVISION-6 Certification

Status: **PASS AS CONCEPT-FORMATION / VOCABULARY-GOVERNANCE REVISION BUILD**

REVISION-6 extends REVISION-5 from restoring participation in interpretation to governing what happens when new concepts and vocabularies themselves enter inquiry.

Certified:

- concepts carry provenance, contributors, evidence, definition, contrast set, scope, and contestability;
- interpretive resources may coexist without a forced winner;
- concept translation may be full-local, partial, structural, task-only, or fail;
- translation remainder remains explicit;
- healthy institutional adoption is not automatically capture;
- semantic change alone is insufficient for co-option;
- capture requires semantic shift plus governance loss such as provenance erasure, contributor displacement, or reduced contestability;
- co-option requires retained label, expanded institutional authority, and capture conditions;
- practical coordination can occur without lexical convergence;
- failed translation does not force synthetic common language;
- apparent convergence produced by concept capture is flagged as distorted;
- no single vocabulary is treated as a universal metalanguage or truth-authorizing language.
