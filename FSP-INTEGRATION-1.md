# FSP-INTEGRATION-1

## Revision-to-Cartography Crosswalk, Seven-View Capability Placement, Research-Only Boundary, and Kernel-Change Gate

**Status:** Integration bridge between the provisional Revision Research Base and the frozen seven-view Cartography shell.

## 1. Purpose

FSP now has two obligations that must not be confused:

1. preserve the **full research encyclopedia**;
2. expose only the amount of structure a human needs for a lived inquiry.

INTEGRATION-1 therefore treats the Revision Research Base as a governed overlay on the existing Cartography shell.

The default rule is:

> No kernel change unless a genuine human-facing representation gap is demonstrated.

## 2. Three placement classes

Every Revision capability is classified as one or more of:

- **USER_VISIBLE** — the human may encounter a compressed ordinary-language form;
- **HIDDEN_GOVERNANCE** — the system may use the capability to preserve invariants or trigger deeper inquiry without displaying the formal research construct;
- **RESEARCH_ONLY_DETAIL** — the full formal object remains available in the encyclopedia/research layer but does not belong in the ordinary seven-view journey.

A capability may belong to more than one class because the compressed cue and the research representation are different artifacts.

## 3. Exposure levels

Human exposure is:

- FULL — ordinary-language form can be central to the view;
- LIGHT — short cue or bounded prompt;
- COMPRESSED — several research distinctions collapse into one ordinary-language question;
- RARE — only triggered when the case demands it;
- NONE — never shown in the ordinary shell.

## 4. Seven-view allocation

### START

Human purpose:

- orient or form the bounded task;
- establish privacy and scope.

Revision overlay:

- mostly hidden constitutional/freeze status;
- no requirement that the user learn FSP constitutional vocabulary.

START should remain cognitively light.

### PEOPLE

Human purpose:

- self;
- maps of other persons;
- report/inference provenance.

Revision overlay can selectively surface:

- credibility distortion;
- unequal access to evidence;
- interpretive exclusion.

Ordinary questions include:

- "What did they actually say?"
- "What is your inference?"
- "Is someone being discounted for reasons unrelated to the evidence?"

### INSTITUTION

Human purpose:

- institutions;
- constraints;
- practical structures.

Revision overlay includes:

- path dependence;
- power/authority distinction;
- concept capture;
- genealogy/sedimentation;
- feedback;
- lock-in;
- capability floors;
- emergency/restoration.

Most of the formal machinery remains hidden or research-only.

### H/S/F

Human purpose:

- historical/inherited relevance;
- situated/current relevance;
- forward/possible relevance.

Revision overlay includes:

- reason change over time;
- genealogy;
- false-friend risk;
- cross-generational capability effects;
- multi-generation REOPEN.

H/S/F remains a relevance locator, not an ontology or verdict.

### CONNECTIONS

Human purpose:

- relations;
- practical power versus authority;
- shared task without manufactured agreement.

Revision overlay includes:

- community convergence/nonconvergence;
- minority reporting;
- evidence asymmetry;
- translation across vocabularies;
- shared-language nonconvergence;
- capability tradeoffs;
- exit/voice/appeal;
- plural authorization.

The central ordinary-language principle is:

> People may coordinate on a bounded shared task without agreeing on everything.

### INVESTIGATE

Human purpose:

- paths;
- unknowns;
- constraints;
- information-generating next actions.

This is the main home of compressed Revision capability:

- revision targets;
- revision reasons;
- translation remainder;
- standing repair;
- counter-public evidence;
- repair windows;
- repair sequencing;
- compensation questions;
- reversible/irreversible repair;
- emergency exception questions.

The user should encounter ordinary questions such as:

- "What might need to change?"
- "What supports this possibility?"
- "What counts against it?"
- "What did not carry across?"
- "What would let this person participate meaningfully?"
- "Is there a real opening for change now?"
- "Can this change be rolled back?"

### RETURN

Human purpose:

- bounded judgment;
- claim ceiling;
- remaining uncertainty;
- next actions;
- REOPEN.

Revision overlay includes:

- revision frontier compression;
- reason pluralism;
- minority preservation;
- historical REOPEN;
- group tradeoffs;
- repair legitimacy;
- plural authorization;
- restoration;
- FSP-as-object REOPEN.

RETURN remains authorial rather than system-decisional.

## 6. Research encyclopedia boundary

The **encyclopedia is retained in full**.

Nothing in this integration requires deletion or compression of:

- formal types;
- audit matrices;
- failed test histories;
- historical cases;
- comparison structures;
- claim ceilings;
- alternative hypotheses;
- research notes.

The human-facing shell is an export profile over the encyclopedia.

Therefore:

```
research richness
!=
required user-visible complexity
```

## 7. Kernel carrier audit

The frozen kernel already provides:

- TASK;
- NODE;
- CLAIM;
- CONNECTION;
- SHARED_TASK;
- CONSTRAINT;
- PATH;
- ACTION;
- MAP_CHANGE;
- RETURN;
- OUTPUT_CARD;
- REOPEN.

These are sufficient to carry the human-facing compressed moves.

Full research objects can remain in a separate RESEARCH_OVERLAY.

## 8. Kernel-change gate

The current gate result is:

`NO_KERNEL_CHANGE`.

Reason:

> No present human-facing Revision capability requires a new persistent kernel object to preserve the seven-view journey.

A future kernel change requires a concrete demonstration that:

1. the capability is genuinely human-facing;
2. it must persist as first-class state;
3. existing carriers lose essential meaning;
4. a research overlay cannot preserve the detail;
5. the change survives kernel invariants and regression.

## 9. Maya / Leila / Jordan vocabulary gate

The decisive usability gate is not whether the shell can compute Revision constructs.

It is:

> Can a human move through the seven views without seeing the research vocabulary?

Maya should encounter:

- "What did your mother actually say?"
- "What is your inference?"
- "Which paths are worth investigating?"
- "What information would change your judgment?"

Leila should encounter:

- "What is the current constraint?"
- "Who can actually affect what happens?"
- "Is that practical power the same as authority?"
- "What alternative paths remain open?"

Jordan should encounter:

- "You do not need a settled task yet."
- "What would you like to explore next?"
- "What are you not claiming yet?"

They should not need to encounter:

- minimal mutilation;
- fecundity;
- hermeneutical injustice;
- Pareto;
- sedimentation;
- constitutional self-audit.

## 10. Acceptance gate

INTEGRATION-1 passes only if:

1. all REVISION-1–12 modules are represented in the crosswalk;
2. all seven Cartography views receive appropriate placements;
3. the kernel-change gate returns NO_KERNEL_CHANGE;
4. Maya, Leila, and Jordan can traverse all seven views;
5. their rendered shell text does not expose the defined research vocabulary;
6. existing seven-view continuity remains green;
7. no kernel semantics are changed.

## 11. Research ceiling

INTEGRATION-1 establishes a placement architecture, not human usability validation.

It does not prove:

- the ordinary-language prompts are optimal;
- progressive disclosure is cognitively light enough;
- the human-facing compression preserves every experiential nuance;
- the research overlay is the final implementation strategy.

Those questions belong to the next human-compression and fidelity builds.

## 12. Disposition

If the acceptance gate passes:

> Treat the Revision Research Base as a full encyclopedia/research overlay above the frozen Cartography kernel, with selectively compressed capabilities allocated to the seven human-facing views.

The next build should then be:

`FSP-INTEGRATION-2 — Human Compression Grammar, Research-Primitive-to-Ordinary-Language Mapping, Progressive Disclosure, and Cognitive-Load Gate.`
