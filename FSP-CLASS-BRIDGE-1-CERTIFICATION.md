## Four consequential workbook moves

1. Map revision pressure.
2. Hold the framework fixed and vary the Vulcan auxiliary.
3. Compare Neptune and Vulcan repair trajectories.
4. State a bounded judgment and REOPEN condition.

The workbook preserves these firewalls:

- anomaly pressure ≠ identified culprit;
- probe result ≠ historical fact;
- shared strategy ≠ guaranteed success;
- rejection of a universal auxiliary-first rule ≠ proof of a universal opposite rule.

## Output card

The output card requires four student-authored fields:

- current bounded claim;
- strongest reason;
- remaining uncertainty;
- REOPEN condition.

No field may be auto-filled from the canonical claim ceiling.

## Journal firewall

Journal use is downstream reflective transfer.

It may ask the learner to connect the inquiry move to another domain, but it may not replace, overwrite, or retroactively edit the bounded judgment produced in the workbook/output card.

## Return contract

The classroom bridge returns from scaffolded operation to learner authorship.

The learner, not the system, supplies the final claim, reason, uncertainty, and REOPEN condition.

## Claim ceiling

This certification establishes a reusable classroom package grammar and one complete Neptune/Vulcan implementation.

It does not establish instructional efficacy, optimal time budget, rubric quality, or transfer success. Those remain empirical and pedagogical questions.
